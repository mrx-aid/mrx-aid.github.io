01

Websites

A landing page, company website or project website with a clear structure and a layout that works across devices.

What I do

  1. 01

    Structure and user journey

    We define the pages, navigation and the visitor’s path to an enquiry, purchase or understanding of the project.

  2. 02

    Design and development

    I create the interface and responsive layout. Forms, a catalogue and other features follow the agreed specification.

  3. 03

    Getting ready to launch

    I check key flows, forms and links, set up basic metadata and help publish the website in the agreed environment.

What you receive

  • A working website with the agreed pages and features.
  • Source files or the project repository.
  • Instructions for launching the website and updating content within the chosen solution.

What I need to get started

Tell me about your goals and audience. References, a page list, copy, images and brand materials are useful if you already have them.

Domains, hosting, licences and content production are agreed separately. Their costs are not automatically included in development.
