The brief

Present recruitment services for manufacturing, construction and logistics companies. Create a clear route from choosing a recruitment model to requesting a proposal, with a separate area for job seekers.

Decisions

01

A business-oriented identity

A blue and graphite palette, large headings and industry photography connect the site to its audience. Light inner-page sections separate content from the dark header.

02

Services organised by need

Cards distinguish specialist search, mass recruitment, shift staffing and onboarding support. Each service has a dedicated detail page.

03

Multiple entry points

Employers can explore services and the company approach before requesting a proposal. Job seekers have a vacancy section, while articles and FAQs offer practical guidance before an enquiry.
