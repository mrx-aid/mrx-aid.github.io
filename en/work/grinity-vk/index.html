The brief

Extend the website’s visual style to its VK community so the company remains recognisable across platforms. Account for viewing the community on desktop and mobile.

Decisions

01

A consistent visual identity

A dark background, vivid green accents and bold typography carry the website’s style across. The cover brings together the headline, team photograph and company contacts.

02

Navigation as part of the design

Four tiles lead to questions, vacancies, case studies and the working process. Outline icons, numbering and a shared composition tie them together.

03

Adapted for mobile

The vertical cover keeps the brand accents and a clear visual hierarchy. Subtle animation adds movement while keeping the headline and contacts in focus.

The result

A complete set of visuals: a cover, avatar, four navigation tiles and an animated mobile version. The shared style connects the website, community and company profile. The case features actual project assets.
