The brief

Turn a former project preview into a personal portfolio while preserving its character: space scenes, the diagonal, the original A mark and animated transitions. Give visitors a clear path from exploring the work to discussing their own project.

Decisions

01

Keep the character

The original logo geometry, changing space scenes and moving diagonal remain. Colours, typography and dark glass panels bring a more consistent visual identity.

02

Organise the content

Selected work, personal projects, service cards, working terms and contract previews now have their place. Contact options open in a dedicated dialog.

03

Refine the details

Mobile buttons fit into the diagonal panel. Russian and English versions, keyboard navigation and reduced motion support complete the interface.

The result

A personal website with a coherent visual identity and practical journeys: explore projects, discover services and get in touch. ATLAS motion is preserved, with localisation and basic search metadata in place. The portfolio continues to grow with real work.
